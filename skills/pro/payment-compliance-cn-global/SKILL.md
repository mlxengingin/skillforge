# 收款路径（国内个人 / 出海）

## When to use
Need to collect money without stalling the product launch.

## Workflow
1. Domestic digital goods: 爱发电 / 闲鱼 / 微信虚拟支付(工具类).
2. Global: Creem / Paddle / Gumroad first; Stripe if entity ready.
3. Prefer one-time payments early to reduce disputes.
4. Keep a second payout rail ready.
5. Document refund policy on the pricing page.

## Guardrails
- Never hardcode keys in the repo.
- Do not promise tax advice; point to a professional when revenue scales.
