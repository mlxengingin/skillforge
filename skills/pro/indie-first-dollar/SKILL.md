# 第一美元路径

## When to use
Owner wants revenue validation within 1–2 weeks with minimal personal stage time.

## Workflow
1. Prefer productized services or digital packs over daily posting.
2. Ship a demo URL same week.
3. Put a real checkout or 爱发电 item online.
4. Do 10 manual outreaches OR 1 marketplace listing — not both half-done.
5. Convert repeated requests into a SKU.

## Guardrails
- Do not design workflows that need daily filming.
- One owner action max for accounts; agent does the rest.
