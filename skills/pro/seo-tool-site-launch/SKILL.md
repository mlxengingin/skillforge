# Indie SEO Tool Site Launch

## When to use
Ship a small paid web tool aimed at Google long-tail keywords.

## Workflow
1. Find 5 long-tail keywords with commercial intent (avoid giant head terms).
2. Draft landing H1, FAQ from People Also Ask, and pricing cards.
3. Scaffold static or Next.js MVP with freemium gate.
4. Add privacy stub + payment placeholder (Creem/Gumroad/爱发电).
5. Submit Search Console; publish 1 real guide page (not spam).
6. First pricing: low one-time fee ($9–$19) before subscription.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent traffic or revenue claims.
- Prefer one-time payment for first launch (fewer disputes).
- Avoid trademark/IP bait keywords.
