# 本地商家 AI 内容代运营周包

## When to use
Package a monthly content service for local shops.

1. Collect real store photos / offers (no fake UGC).
2. Build 7-day calendar: 4 posts + 1 offer + 1 review reply bank.
3. Deliver captions, cover prompts, and weekly metrics sheet.
4. Quote ¥149–¥1998/mo depending on volume (cite public comps).
5. Refuse fake reviews / fake foot traffic tactics.

## Guardrails
