# AI 内容标识合规

## When to use
Publishing AI-assisted content on CN platforms.

## Workflow
1. Check 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 effective 2025-09-01.
2. Enable platform AI declaration (Douyin 发文助手 / Xiaohongshu AI label).
3. Avoid fully automated unmanaged accounts (XHS banned 800k+ AI托管 in 2026).
4. Keep a human review step before publish.
5. Log which assets were AI-generated.

## Guardrails
- No account farming.
- No fake engagement.
