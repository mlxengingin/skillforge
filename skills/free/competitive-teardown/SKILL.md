# Competitive Teardown

## When to use
Research rivals before shipping a paid micro-product.

## Workflow
1. List 5 competitors with public URLs and access dates.
2. Capture feature bullets, pricing tiers, and acquisition channel.
3. Mark gaps you can ship in one week.
4. Write a one-page differentiation claim (who / job / unique wedge).
5. Define the first paid SKU and a 7-day success metric.

## Guardrails
- Cite sources with dates.
- Do not invent MRR or user counts.
- Prefer shippable wedges over feature parity.
